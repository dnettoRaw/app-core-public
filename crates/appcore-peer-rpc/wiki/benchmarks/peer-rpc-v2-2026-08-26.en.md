## AC-014 binary codec comparison

The clean release-profile run at `6f3bc38b4102aeb9f167fe66f62909416f7f3ea1`
compared exact incompressible open/chunk/commit bodies. Body totals exclude
HTTP/TLS headers; the buffer column is the maximum encoded body plus decoded
chunk simultaneously owned by the codec.

| 64 KiB | 88,465 | 66,016 | 25% | 98.333 µs | 6.625 µs | 93% | 153,199 / 131,208 B |

Every codec and subsystem budget passed. Whole-suite peak RSS was 306,448 KiB.
The stable V1 JSON fixtures and canonical V2 JSON/base64 shape remained
unchanged; platform artifacts are still required for a deployable claim.

This evidence certifies the post-1.0 development line. It does not publish V2
or enable it on a V1 host.
